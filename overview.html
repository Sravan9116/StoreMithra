The Cooperative Architecture

How StoreMithra Solves the Kirana Stockout Crisis

Small retail merchants in India lose up to 15% of daily revenue because of supplier delivery delays and limited backroom shelf space. StoreMithra transforms isolated Kiranas into a resilient local network.

1. Hyperlocal Stock Pooling

Neighbor stores within 400m to 1200m share buffer inventories. If your store runs low on high-demand essentials (like Aashirvaad Atta or Fortune Oil), stock is dispatched from a neighboring partner store with surplus in under 15 minutes.

  • 1.5km geofenced safety radius
  • Full privacy: no financial ledger exposure
  • Perishable transit distance caps (1200m)

2. Censored Demand AI Forecasting

Traditional POS systems severely under-forecast because they only count transactions—recording 0 sales when a shelf is empty. StoreMithra’s 14-day rolling average model adjusts for stockout censorship and supplier lead times.

  • Dynamic confidence decay (e.g. 5-day unverified = 45%)
  • Predicts depletion 2 to 3 days in advance
  • Barcode scan & quick sale confidence reset

3. Instant UPI Money Math & Trust

Every decision compares money math: Avoided lost sales vs supplier delay vs neighbor transfer fee. Fulfillments route automatically through merchant UPI IDs with transparent settlement ledgering and reputation scoring.

  • Instant merchant-to-merchant UPI routing
  • Transparent transfer fee economics (₹25 - ₹40)
  • Fulfiller trust score increases with on-time deliveries
